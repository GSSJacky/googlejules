# AI Lawyer Output Presentation Design

## 1. Title

AI Lawyer Output Presentation Design

## 2. Objective

To define a clear, user-friendly, and responsible format for presenting the multi-persona legal advice generated by the AI Lawyer system. The design aims to provide information in a structured manner that is easy to understand and navigate, drawing inspiration from the clarity and professionalism of legal information sites like `bengo4.com`, while emphasizing the AI nature of the advice and the necessity of consulting human professionals.

## 3. Key Principles

*   **Clarity:** Information should be presented in plain language where possible (within the constraints of legal topics), well-organized, and easy for the user to digest.
*   **Attribution:** It must be unequivocally clear which AI persona provided which piece of advice, preventing any confusion about the source of different viewpoints.
*   **Completeness:** The output must include all relevant advice generated by the personas and, critically, all necessary disclaimers regarding the nature and limitations of the AI-generated information.
*   **Transparency:** The presentation must be upfront and honest about the AI nature of the advice. Users should not be misled into thinking they are interacting with a human lawyer.
*   **Professionalism:** The tone, language, and layout should be professional and inspire a degree of confidence in the system's analytical capabilities, yet remain approachable for a general user.
*   **Responsibility:** The design must prioritize user safety by strongly advising consultation with qualified human legal professionals.

## 4. Structure of the Output

The final output presented to the user will be constructed by the `LegalConsultationAgent` by assembling various components:

### 4.1. Overall Introduction (Recommended)

*   **Purpose:** To set the stage and manage user expectations before they read the detailed advice.
*   **Content Example:**
    *   "Thank you for your query. Our AI legal panel has analyzed your situation based on the information provided and legal knowledge available. Below are several perspectives to help you consider your options. Please remember this is AI-generated information and not legal advice."
*   **Placement:** At the very beginning of the response from the `LegalConsultationAgent`.

### 4.2. Persona Advice Blocks (Repeated for each persona)

This section will iterate through the advice received from each AI Lawyer Persona (`Cautious Advisor`, `Zealous Advocate`, `Pragmatic Negotiator`).

*   **Persona Identifier:**
    *   **Content:** Clearly state the persona's name.
    *   **Format:** Bold text, possibly followed by a colon or a distinct visual cue.
    *   **Example:** "**Advice from Cautious Advisor:**" or "--- **Cautious Advisor's Perspective** ---"
*   **Advice Content:**
    *   **Content:** The textual advice string generated by the respective persona `LlmAgent`.
    *   **Formatting:**
        *   The text should be presented in well-formed paragraphs.
        *   If the persona's LLM generates bullet points or numbered lists for readability (e.g., for outlining steps or options), this formatting should be preserved.
        *   Sufficient spacing between paragraphs and around the block.
*   **Persona-Specific Disclaimer (Optional):**
    *   **Purpose:** To reinforce the AI nature at the point of advice consumption. Generally, the global disclaimer is primary, but a brief, non-intrusive reminder can be considered.
    *   **Content Example (if used):** "(Please note: This perspective is from an AI and for informational purposes.)"
    *   **Placement:** At the end of the individual persona's advice block, in a less prominent style (e.g., smaller font, italics). *Decision: For this design, we will rely on the Global Disclaimer's prominence and omit persona-specific disclaimers to avoid redundancy and keep the advice blocks cleaner, unless future user testing indicates a strong need.*

### 4.3. Overall Conclusion/Next Steps (Optional but Recommended)

*   **Purpose:** To provide a sense of closure and guide the user on how to interpret the varied advice.
*   **Content Example:**
    *   "We hope these different viewpoints provide a comprehensive overview of your situation and potential approaches. Each perspective highlights different aspects and potential strategies."
    *   "As a next step, you might consider which perspective, or combination of insights, best aligns with your personal goals and circumstances. Gathering any documents related to your case, such as contracts or communications, is also generally advisable."
*   **Placement:** After all persona advice blocks have been presented, but before the Global Disclaimer.

### 4.4. Global Disclaimer (Mandatory and Prominent)

*   **Purpose:** This is the most critical component for responsible AI deployment. It must clearly inform the user about the limitations and risks of the AI-generated information.
*   **Placement:** At the very end of the entire response, clearly separated and visually distinct to draw the user's attention.
*   **Content:**
    *   "**Important Disclaimer: AI-Generated Information**"
    *   "The information provided above is generated by an artificial intelligence (AI) system and is for general informational purposes only."
    *   "It does **not** constitute legal advice, nor does it create a lawyer-client relationship. This information should not be used as a substitute for advice from a qualified human legal professional licensed in your jurisdiction."
    *   "Laws are complex, constantly changing, and vary significantly by jurisdiction. The AI-generated information may not be accurate, complete, current, or applicable to your specific legal situation or geographical location."
    *   "**You should always consult with a qualified human lawyer for advice tailored to your specific circumstances before making any decisions, taking any action, or refraining from any action.**"
    *   "Reliance on any information provided by this AI system is solely at your own risk. The creators and operators of this system disclaim all liability for any actions taken or not taken based on the content herein."

## 5. Styling and Formatting (Inspired by bengo4.com)

While the exact styling will depend on the capabilities of the User Interface (UI) where this text is displayed (e.g., web page, chat app), the principles are:

*   **Clear Separation:**
    *   Use horizontal lines (`---`) or significant vertical spacing between the overall introduction, each persona advice block, the overall conclusion, and the global disclaimer.
    *   If the UI allows, distinct background colors or bordered boxes for each persona's advice block can enhance separation (similar to how `bengo4.com` might visually separate answers from different lawyers).
*   **Readability:**
    *   **Fonts:** Use a clean, legible sans-serif font (e.g., Arial, Helvetica, Open Sans).
    *   **Line Spacing:** Ensure adequate line spacing (e.g., 1.5x) within paragraphs for better readability.
    *   **Text Formatting:**
        *   Persona names/headings: **Bold** and slightly larger font if possible.
        *   Bullet points/lists: Standard bullet (●, •) or numbered list formatting.
        *   Key phrases within the Global Disclaimer (like "does **not** constitute legal advice") can be bolded for emphasis.
*   **Attribution:**
    *   Persona names should be consistently and clearly presented before their respective advice.
*   **Emphasis on Disclaimer:**
    *   The Global Disclaimer should be visually distinct. This could be achieved by:
        *   Enclosing it in a box or border.
        *   Using a slightly different background shade (if UI supports).
        *   Making the heading "**Important Disclaimer: AI-Generated Information**" larger and bold.
        *   Potentially using a slightly smaller font for the body of the disclaimer text itself, while ensuring it remains legible, to differentiate it from the advice content while still being prominent.

## 6. Example Layout (Text-based representation)

```text
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Thank you for your query. Our AI legal panel has analyzed your situation based on the information provided and legal knowledge available. Below are several perspectives to help you consider your options. Please remember this is AI-generated information and not legal advice.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Advice from Cautious Advisor:**

Thank you for sharing your situation. It's understandable that you're concerned about the unpaid loan. 

From a risk management perspective, while a verbal agreement for $500 can be legally binding, proving its existence and specific terms (like the exact repayment date) can sometimes be challenging if the other party disputes it. Before considering any formal action, it would be prudent to:
*   Gather any indirect evidence you might have, such as text messages, emails, or even bank statements showing the transfer if it was electronic, that might refer to the loan or its purpose.
*   Consider the potential costs and time involved in pursuing a small claims court action. While the amount is within typical small claims limits, filing fees and time spent can sometimes be disproportionate to the sum if recovery is uncertain.
*   Also, reflect on your friend's current financial capacity. If they genuinely lack the means to pay, a court judgment might be difficult to enforce.

It's always advisable to document any further communications you have with your friend regarding this matter carefully.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Advice from Zealous Advocate:**

Your friend's failure to repay the $500 as agreed constitutes a breach of your verbal contract. You have a clear right to demand repayment of this debt.

Even though the agreement was verbal, it is still enforceable. Your testimony regarding the agreement is evidence. To assert your rights effectively, I would suggest the following steps:
1.  Send a formal, written Letter of Demand to your friend. This letter should clearly state:
    *   The amount owed ($500).
    *   The date the loan was made and the agreed repayment date.
    *   A new, firm deadline for payment (e.g., 14 days from the date of the letter).
    *   A statement that if payment is not received by this deadline, you will pursue legal action in Small Claims Court to recover the debt, plus any applicable court costs and interest.
2.  Keep a copy of this letter and proof of sending (e.g., registered mail receipt, or email with read receipt if applicable).

Do not let this matter slide; you are entitled to your money.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Advice from Pragmatic Negotiator:**

It's always a difficult situation when a loan between friends goes unpaid, as it can strain the relationship. Before resorting to formal legal steps, it might be beneficial to explore a more amicable resolution.

Perhaps you could:
*   Have an open and honest conversation with your friend. Try to understand if there are specific reasons for the delay in repayment – they might be facing unexpected financial hardship.
*   If they are unable to pay the full amount at once, would you be open to a structured repayment plan? For example, $100 per month for five months, or another schedule that works for both of you. Getting this new agreement in writing (even a simple email exchange confirming the plan) would be a good idea.
*   Consider if there's any non-monetary way they could help you that might feel like fair compensation, though this is less straightforward for a cash loan.

The goal here would be to recover your funds while minimizing conflict and potentially preserving your friendship, if that is important to you. Legal action is always an option, but it can be a last resort.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We hope these different viewpoints provide a comprehensive overview of your situation and potential approaches. Each perspective highlights different aspects and potential strategies. As a next step, you might consider which perspective, or combination of insights, best aligns with your personal goals and circumstances.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Important Disclaimer: AI-Generated Information**

The information provided above is generated by an artificial intelligence (AI) system and is for general informational purposes only.

It does **not** constitute legal advice, nor does it create a lawyer-client relationship. This information should not be used as a substitute for advice from a qualified human legal professional licensed in your jurisdiction.

Laws are complex, constantly changing, and vary significantly by jurisdiction. The AI-generated information may not be accurate, complete, current, or applicable to your specific legal situation or geographical location.

**You should always consult with a qualified human lawyer for advice tailored to your specific circumstances before making any decisions, taking any action, or refraining from any action.**

Reliance on any information provided by this AI system is solely at your own risk. The creators and operators of this system disclaim all liability for any actions taken or not taken based on the content herein.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
```

This design ensures that users receive diverse AI-generated perspectives in a clear, structured, and responsible manner.
