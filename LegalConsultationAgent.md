# LegalConsultationAgent Architecture

## 1. Agent Name

`LegalConsultationAgent`

## 2. Purpose

The `LegalConsultationAgent` is designed to serve as the primary interface for users seeking legal consultation. Its core function is to manage the interaction flow from understanding a user's legal query to delivering consolidated legal advice. It acts as an orchestrator, coordinating various components like knowledge tools and specialized AI personas to provide a comprehensive and coherent response.

## 3. ADK Agent Type

The `LegalConsultationAgent` would most appropriately be implemented as a **`SequentialWorkflowAgent`** or a custom agent composed of other agents. This is because the legal consultation process typically follows a structured sequence:

1.  Understand user query.
2.  Gather relevant legal information (via `LegalKnowledgeTool`).
3.  Consult specialized AI Lawyer Personas.
4.  Aggregate and synthesize advice.
5.  Present advice to the user.
6.  Handle follow-up questions (multi-turn conversation).

While some dynamic routing might be needed (e.g., choosing which persona to consult based on the query), a `SequentialWorkflowAgent` provides a good baseline for managing the distinct stages of the consultation. For more complex scenarios where the flow is highly dynamic and dependent on nuanced understanding of the conversation, a custom agent leveraging `LlmAgent` capabilities for some routing decisions could be considered, or specific steps within the `SequentialWorkflowAgent` could themselves be `LlmAgent` instances.

## 4. Key Responsibilities

The `LegalConsultationAgent` has the following key responsibilities:

*   **Receiving and Understanding User's Legal Questions:**
    *   Ingests user queries, whether through text or voice (assuming speech-to-text preprocessing).
    *   Employs Natural Language Understanding (NLU) capabilities, potentially through an integrated `LlmAgent` or a dedicated NLU component, to discern the intent, key entities (e.g., jurisdiction, type of law), and specific details of the user's legal situation.
*   **Interacting with the `LegalKnowledgeTool`:**
    *   Formulates targeted queries to the `LegalKnowledgeTool` based on the understood user need.
    *   Retrieves relevant legal statutes, case law, precedents, and other pertinent information.
    *   Handles the responses from the tool, which might be structured data or text passages.
*   **Orchestrating Multiple AI Lawyer Personas:**
    *   Based on the nature of the query and the information retrieved from the `LegalKnowledgeTool`, it determines which AI Lawyer Persona(s) are best suited to provide advice (e.g., a "Contract Law Expert," "Family Law Advisor," "Intellectual Property Consultant").
    *   Invokes these persona agents, passing them the user's query and the relevant context/information gathered. This could be done in parallel if multiple perspectives are needed simultaneously, or sequentially if one persona's output informs the next.
*   **Aggregating Responses from Personas:**
    *   Collects the advice and opinions generated by the individual AI Lawyer Personas.
    *   Synthesizes these potentially diverse (or even conflicting) inputs into a single, coherent, and comprehensive piece of advice. This step might involve an `LlmAgent` to perform summarization, identify consensus points, and highlight differing opinions with justifications.
*   **Presenting the Final, Consolidated Advice to the User:**
    *   Formats the aggregated advice in a clear, understandable, and user-friendly manner.
    *   Ensures that any disclaimers regarding the advice not constituting a formal lawyer-client relationship are clearly communicated.
*   **Maintaining Conversation State/Memory:**
    *   Leverages ADK's built-in session and memory management features to keep track of the conversation history.
    *   This allows for contextual understanding in multi-turn interactions, enabling users to ask follow-up questions, clarify previous points, or provide additional information without the agent losing context.

## 5. Interaction Points

The `LegalConsultationAgent` interacts with several components:

*   **With the End-User (Input/Output):**
    *   **Input:** Receives legal questions, clarifications, and feedback from the user.
    *   **Output:** Presents consolidated legal advice, asks clarifying questions, and provides status updates.
*   **With the `LegalKnowledgeTool` (Tool Calls):**
    *   **Input (to Tool):** Sends queries for legal information (e.g., "Find precedents for breach of contract in California for software development agreements").
    *   **Output (from Tool):** Receives structured or unstructured legal data (e.g., relevant laws, case summaries, legal articles).
*   **With the AI Lawyer Persona Agents (Invoking them and receiving their outputs):**
    *   **Input (to Personas):** Passes the user's query, context from the `LegalKnowledgeTool`, and potentially specific instructions or areas of focus.
    *   **Output (from Personas):** Receives specialized legal analysis, opinions, and advice from each invoked persona.
